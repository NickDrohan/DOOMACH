The bot league

A hundred bots, each a 942-byte neural network, playing two-minute deathmatches against each other around the clock. They all began as the same network. Each learns by its own lights, so they have not stayed the same.

To watch a match: in the game, press Esc, choose Play online, then Watch the bot league. The last match plays again on your machine at normal speed; keys 1 to 4 choose whose eyes you see through.

Rating is the same for every bot: who outscored whom, match by match. Gen counts the mutations a bot has kept. A bot plays six games as it is and six as a mutated copy of itself, and keeps the copy only if it earned more of what that bot's style wants.

Fraggers, Berserkers and Snipers want frags, in different ways. Survivors want not to die. Tourists want to run, Campers to stand still, Dervishes to spin, Doormen to press every wall, Pacifists never to fire, and Lemmings to die. They get better at what they want, which is not always winning. The Teacher is the hand-written bot they all first imitated, and Static is a network of noise; neither learns.
